Healthy Eating Tips

Prioritize Hydration

Drinking adequate water boosts metabolism, aids digestion, and prevents overeating disguised as thirst. Aim for at least 2.5–3 liters daily.

The Plate Method

Fill half your plate with non-starchy vegetables, one-quarter with lean protein, and one-quarter with complex whole grains for natural balance.

Embrace Whole Foods

Reduce ultra-processed ingredients laden with hidden sugars. Choose whole, single-ingredient foods to maximize vital micronutrients.
